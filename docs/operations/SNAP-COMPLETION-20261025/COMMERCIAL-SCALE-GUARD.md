# Snapchat campaign commercial proof before scaling

The execution gate previously treated trusted provider-window attribution as sufficient for a scale action. That proves the origin of Snapchat delivery/conversion metrics, not exact store orders or product costs. The separate live store-level profit envelope remains necessary, but cannot prove a specific campaign's commercial cohort.

The existing unified execution reader now creates a separate `commercial_scale` proof from its freshly reloaded campaign report. For child entities it uses the exact same-account, same-period parent campaign and labels that campaign as the source; it does not allocate parent sales to an ad or ad squad. The proof requires complete exact-campaign commerce attribution, matching financial-order counts and sales, complete recorded costs (including explicitly recorded Salla fallback provenance), zero missing-cost orders, finite SAR amounts, matching campaign ad spend, consistent contribution arithmetic, and a fresh account observation. Existing source fingerprints bind these facts and the parent to the original candidate and detect changes before execution.

Only Snapchat `scale` requires this proof. Pause and reduce retain their existing safety checks. Legacy evidence without commercial proof fails closed for scaling. No profit-sign threshold is added: the model retains marketing judgment, while deterministic code checks whether the facts are complete. This is campaign contribution profit, before shipping/payment/operating allocations; it is not represented as campaign net profit. The existing separately recalculated store net-profit completeness guard still runs before writes.

## Follow-up — upstream commerce freshness is not yet provable

`snapchat_v2/salla_outcomes.py` reads the local `unified_orders` cohort and loads its cost context at request time, but neither it nor `UnifiedCommerceOutcomes` / `UnifiedCommerceProfitability` exports an authoritative Salla import watermark, source-range completeness timestamp, or calculator revision/time bound to that cohort. The older exact-match profitability adapter has a five-minute process cache keyed by product-cost revision; a recent calculation/cache time still cannot prove upstream orders are current. An order's `updated_at` cannot prove missing orders have been imported. Snapchat account sync freshness must not be described as commerce freshness.

Until a real typed upstream contract carries and validates that provenance, Snapchat scale is explicitly fail-closed with `campaign_commerce_freshness_unproven`. The commercial proof reports `commerce_freshness.status=unproven` and a null `observed_at`; the separate Snapchat timestamp is named `provider_observed_at`. No local clock, arbitrary row field, or provider timestamp is promoted into store freshness. Current production data therefore cannot authorize scale through this new proof even when the locally calculated cost/cohort is complete. Pause/reduce, analysis, approval preparation, and outcome measurement retain their existing behavior.

Required upstream completion: establish a tenant-bound successful Salla synchronization watermark and exact period/population coverage (including absent-order detection), bind the locally calculated commerce cohort and cost revision to that run, expose it through the typed unified contract, then validate chronology, age, identity and window at candidate capture and again before execution. Updating that contract and producer requires a follow-up change; no dormant ad-hoc timestamp field can activate scaling.
