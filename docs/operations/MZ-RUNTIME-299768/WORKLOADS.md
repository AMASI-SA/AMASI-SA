# Dashboard and advertising workload inventory

Source snapshot for [issue #1724](https://github.com/AMASI-SA/AMASI-SA/issues/1724): `8ec4f1aaa2449369b402d6c1985ed761c52c739a`. The switches below are code defaults. Effective environment values, account policies and deployed process ownership require separate runtime evidence.

## Scheduled entry points

`server.py` mounts the integrations and Ads Manager routers. Their startup hooks add timers in **each web replica**. Looking only at the tasks listed in `_local_startup` misses these schedulers. The timers below start after local readiness, except for the separate CAPI boundary described below. [Integrations router](../../../backend/integrations_control_center/__init__.py) · [Snapchat reporting registration](../../../backend/integrations_control_center/snapchat_native_tracking_routes.py) · [Ads Manager router](../../../backend/ads_manager/__init__.py)

| Workload | Default launcher and cadence | Work and ownership boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native Meta, Snapchat, TikTok and Google reporting | `MEZAN_ADS_AUTO_SYNC_ENABLED=true` starts an asyncio timer in web. Initial delay 45 s, due interval 300 s, lease polling 15 s. Connected targets and provider gates determine actual work. | A Mongo scheduler lease selects the replica. In that replica, bounded tasks acquire the process governor, call reporting APIs and persist facts/run state. The rolling window defaults to two days and extends to at least three for Meta/Snap. Snapchat additionally leases its refresh and stages canonical performance fact writes. Any handoff must preserve fact ownership, date windows, deduplication and incomplete coverage. [Scheduler](../../../backend/integrations_control_center/ads_auto_sync_scheduler.py) |

## Interactive work that can compete with employees

- Opening the dashboard or changing its period starts an owner-scoped summary read and a saved platform-spend read. A spend-proof mismatch can trigger a second summary calculation. The dashboard also refreshes the summary every 30 s, on order changes, focus, visibility and reconnection. PR #1726 gates a background read behind a pending read in the same mounted dashboard. Other tabs and replicas still submit work. The live orders feed is independently owned by `useOrders`. [Page](../../../frontend/src/pages/AdvancedDashboard.jsx) · [Refresh settings](../../../frontend/src/lib/dashboardLiveRefresh.js)
- The spend card has its own 5-min saved-data poll. GA realtime polls every 60 s, the shadow comparison follows date changes, and abandoned-cart automatic reconciliation is frozen by default. Preserve source freshness and independently check these reads when testing the dashboard under load. [Spend card](../../../frontend/src/components/DashboardAdsSpendCard.jsx)
- The summary acquires `governor.heavy("dashboard")`, calls the legacy summary and reads selected-period orders plus current-month orders if the ranges differ. Later stages resolve product/bundle costs, advertising, and recurring costs. The weighted governor defaults to two global permits **per Python process**: a dashboard or startup task costs two and an ad task costs one. Cooperative CPU checkpoints yield between units. Neither mechanism reserves dedicated CPU or Mongo capacity for employee requests. [Summary](../../../backend/dashboard_v2_routes.py) · [Governor](../../../backend/resource_governor.py) · [CPU checkpoints](../../../backend/dashboard_cpu_budget.py)

The managed-worker decision needs the platform's service lifecycle, resource limits and recovery contract for this project. For a Preview comparison, record the owners and timing of both native and shadow sync, the summary read, Mongo waits, event-loop lag and employee-route p95/p99 under the same load. The owner-only `/api/integrations-v2/ads-auto-sync/status` exposes native cycle timing and tenant-scoped outcomes. A scheduler row or successful health probe alone cannot establish the duration of a supplier assignment.
