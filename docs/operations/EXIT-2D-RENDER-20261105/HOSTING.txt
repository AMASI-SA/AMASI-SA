PID1 supervises Web API, SMTP capture and nginx: one child exiting fails the
whole Web service and terminates siblings; SIGTERM drains for <=20s then kills
remaining children, inside Render's configured30s. Workers retain existing15s
role leases,3s heartbeat and bounded6s drain. A SIGKILL keeps its lease until TTL;
an immediate replacement correctly refuses ownership and supervisor retry waits.
Render can overlap old/new instances during deployment even with numInstances=1.
Do not treat that setting as proof of a singleton writer. For writer updates,
stop/suspend the existing writer through the control plane, prove its entire
process tree is gone, then deploy/resume its replacement. A new instance may
correctly refuse the existing role claim; do not delete that claim to force a
rolling deploy. Active provider calls require reconciliation and supervisor
termination evidence, not merely lease expiry. This is a controlled writer
handoff with a pause, not a claim of zero-downtime/exactly-once provider execution.

  compound sparse unique event-request index: Mongo indexes the document whenever
  user_id exists even if client_request_id is missing. New ready events receive
  stable supplier-ready:<dispatch-id> request identity. The load checks ten distinct
  dispatches and sequential replays, requiring one ready event per dispatch.
  No invoice, financial policy, provider write or Production repair is included.
  No historical events are rewritten or backfilled. Concurrent ready-action audit
  repair beyond the existing handler's behavior remains outside this small fix.
